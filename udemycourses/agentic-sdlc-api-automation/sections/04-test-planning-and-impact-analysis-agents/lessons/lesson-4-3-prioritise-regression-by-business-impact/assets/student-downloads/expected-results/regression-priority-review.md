# Expected result — Lesson 4.3

Use a transparent prioritisation model. For the exercise, score each dimension from 1 (low) to 5 (high):
- Business impact: 40%
- Change proximity: 30%
- Blast radius: 20%
- Failure likelihood: 10%

Weighted score = (impact × 0.40) + (proximity × 0.30) + (blast radius × 0.20) + (likelihood × 0.10).

The likelihood score is reviewer judgement, not a measured probability. Record the reason and confidence. Do not present the score as an objective risk measurement.

## Expected ordering
1. Patient self-cancellation rule and exact time boundary.
2. Ownership/privacy boundary.
3. Patient appointment creation as broad lifecycle regression.
4. Patient read-after-cancel and staff cancellation as tied-priority regression concerns.
5. Patient rescheduling.
6. Staff downstream lifecycle and public discovery smoke coverage.

The exact order between adjacent journeys may change when the learner cites stronger evidence. A strong answer explains the trade-off and names what is excluded from the first regression pass.

## Common failure
Agent Mode ranks tests by endpoint count or convenience. Reject rankings without a business-impact rationale and source evidence.
